Fun meets formal.
A brand that refuses to be ordinary.

Cafe Catalyst is a young, urban coffee space in New Delhi targeting professionals who want more from a café — a place to meet, work, think, and belong. The owner came with a clear ambition: fun and urban in energy, luxurious and professional in finish. Not brown. Not conventional.

The brief covered brand identity from scratch — logo, colour system, typography, illustration, iconography, print collateral, packaging, and digital UI — all delivered solo in four days.

The Brief

Two creative registers, one brand

Challenge
Fun meets formal
Incorporating a fun, trendy, urbanistic vibe into the same design as elegance, professionalism and luxury. These two aesthetic registers normally cancel each other out. The task was to make them coexist without compromise.
Solution
Curves with discipline
Curvy hand-crafted illustrations for warmth and personality, counterbalanced by rigorous minimalism and clean grids. A curvy sans paired with a sleek tall serif. Unconventional brand colours chosen deliberately to break from café brown and make a bold market statement.
Research

Against convention

Studied nationally and globally established café brands. Key breakthrough: nearly every café brand reaches for brown. Taking inspiration from Lloyds Bank — a financial institution that uses green to stand apart in a sea of blue — the decision was made to use violet as the primary brand colour. Not coffee. Not warm neutrals. Something immediately identifiable and owned.

Illustration System

Drawn by hand

A custom illustration library created for the brand — characters, icons and scenes that bring the café's personality to life across every surface. The style is intentionally expressive: loose lines, bold fills, just enough detail to feel premium without feeling rigid.
Characters & Scenes
Brand illustration library
A set of original characters — a woman at a café table, a barista, a coffee drinker — drawn in the brand's purple and yellow, placed across packaging, digital surfaces and in-store materials.
Iconography
Custom icon set — two colourways
Four bespoke icons drawn for the brand: bread, coffee cup, milkshake and wifi. Produced in gold for warm applications and black for print and dark surfaces.
Packaging

The brand in your hands

The cup sleeve, takeaway bag and poster are where the illustration system earns its keep. A tiled pattern of brand illustrations in violet on gold turns every piece of packaging into a brand statement.
Digital Design

Every screen, on-brand

The brand system extended fully into digital — a website homepage and mobile app UI, both using the same visual language: yellow as primary canvas, violet as the interaction colour, illustrations as background texture.

Learnings

You can't connect the dots looking forward

1
A tight system makes speed possible
Working across eight deliverable types in four days demonstrated that a disciplined brand system is not a constraint — it is what makes speed possible. When the rules are clear, every decision is faster.
2
Stay committed to the direction, not the spec
The logo came out completely different from the first brief iteration. The client loved it and stuck with it. Sometimes it is genuinely difficult to understand how things will take shape before they are actually finished.
3
Break conventions with conviction, not randomness
Choosing violet over brown was a researched strategic decision inspired by how Lloyds Bank used green to own its category. Unconventional choices land when they are grounded in logic.
