Littletags' first offline trunk show — no playbook, one week, forty brands.

Trunk Show was a major offline retail activation — Littletags' first time running one at this scale. Over two days, the brand offered flat discounts on 40+ international labels at a physical venue, backed by a full campaign across print, digital, and in-store touchpoints.

I led the design team on this project, overseeing creative direction end-to-end and directly managing three designers through the full production cycle — from briefing and brainstorming to final delivery.

The work

Bringing the identity to life across every touchpoint.

With the wordmark and logo lockup defined, the identity was carried across the website, marketing collateral, and every physical and digital touchpoint of the event.

Logo concept development — construction grid and final lockup.
Website homepage redesign — hero banner announcing the new DLF Avenue, Saket location.
Campaign emailer and "Brands in Focus" collateral, spotlighting partner labels.
Social media ad set — consistent layout system adapted across partner brand creatives.
Discount voucher coupons distributed in-store and to existing customers.
Save-the-date / thank-you card sent to customers ahead of the event.
